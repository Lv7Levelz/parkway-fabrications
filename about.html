About

Parkway Fabrications is a Sheffield-based metal fabrication specialist combining advanced machinery with deep industry experience. Its current website describes a workforce with nearly 100 years of combined fabrication and manufacturing experience.

A practical manufacturing partner

From individual components to complex fabricated assemblies, Parkway's service model is built around understanding the specification, choosing the right process and delivering finished metalwork to the required standard.

Capability under one roof

  • CNC folding
  • MIG & TIG welding
  • Perforated sheet products
  • Bespoke fabrication
  • Project support from concept to completion
