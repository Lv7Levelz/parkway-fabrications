Parkway Fabrications is a Sheffield-based metal fabrication and manufacturing company serving local, UK and international customers across industrial screens, perforated sheet, laser cutting and bespoke fabrication.

Industrial heritage, modern equipment

Manufacturing that solves real problems.

The existing Parkway site focuses heavily on long-life industrial screens, custom fabrication and the ability to manufacture around customer specifications — including non-standard machines and difficult one-off projects.

For screen customers, advance production and call-off ordering can reduce downtime when unexpected damage occurs. For bespoke fabrication customers, the company positions itself around design-to-installation problem solving and a broad materials/process toolkit.
