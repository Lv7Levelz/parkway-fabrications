Questions buyers ask

What information is needed for a folding quote?

Supply material grade and thickness, dimensions, bend angles and radii, quantity and any critical tolerances. A 3D model plus dimensioned PDF is especially useful.

Does a 4m bed mean every 4m part can be folded?

Not automatically. Tonnage, tooling, material, geometry and bend position all affect feasibility. Parkway must review the specific drawing.

Can Parkway cut and fold the same components?

Yes. Laser cutting and CNC folding can be reviewed as a connected route, reducing the need to coordinate separate suppliers.
