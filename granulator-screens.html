Questions buyers ask

What does Parkway need to quote a replacement screen?

Provide the machine make and model as a reference, plus a drawing or sample, overall dimensions, curvature, mounting details, hole shape and size, pitch, material, thickness and quantity.

How do hole size and open area affect a screen?

They influence product sizing, throughput and remaining metal between apertures. The correct combination depends on the process and equipment; Parkway does not prescribe operating settings without the application details.

Can compatibility be confirmed from a machine name alone?

No. Model variants and previous modifications can change fit. An existing screen or complete dimensional drawing is the safest basis for review.

Are wear-resistant materials available?

Parkway publicly references hardened wear plate and specialist material options. Suitability and availability must be confirmed against the duty and drawing.
