After profiling, parts can move to CNC folding and sheet-metal bending, then MIG or TIG welding or complete metal fabrication. When the scope is ready, send the complete RFQ to Parkway.

Questions buyers ask

What files can I send for a laser-cutting quote?

DXF, STEP and PDF are useful review formats. Include dimensions, material grade, thickness and quantity; Parkway will confirm whether the supplied file is suitable for manufacture.

Can laser-cut parts also be folded or welded?

Yes. Parkway offers CNC folding, welding and fabrication, so a drawing can be reviewed for multiple stages rather than cutting alone.

What tolerances and thicknesses are available?

These limits are drawing-, material- and geometry-dependent. Parkway has not published a universal tolerance or thickness table, so the team will confirm what is achievable during review.
