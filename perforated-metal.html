Questions buyers ask

What determines a perforated sheet specification?

Aperture shape and size, pitch, pattern, open area, material, thickness, blank margins, flatness and any forming or finishing all affect the result.

Can perforated sheet be folded and welded?

Parkway offers folding and welding alongside perforated metal. Feasibility depends on hole layout, edge margins and bend positions, so supply the finished-part drawing.

Can Parkway copy an existing perforated component?

An existing screen or accurate drawing can provide useful reference dimensions. Parkway will confirm the information required and will not assume machine compatibility.
