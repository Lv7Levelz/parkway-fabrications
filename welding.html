Questions buyers ask

Which welding processes are available?

Parkway publicly references MIG, TIG and specialist welding. The appropriate process depends on material, joint design and project requirements.

What quality checks are carried out?

Parkway’s current published information states that every weld receives a 100% post-weld quality check to meet ISO standards. Project-specific inspection and documentation should be stated in the RFQ.

What should I include on a welding enquiry?

Include material grade, thickness, assembly drawings, weld symbols, quantities, finish and any required standard, inspection or documentation.
